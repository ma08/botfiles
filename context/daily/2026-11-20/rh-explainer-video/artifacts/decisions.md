# Decisions Sourya made

The agent proposed, Sourya decided. Every choice below was a multiple-choice question with a recommended option, asked one at a time.

## Before any planning

- **Voice:** picked from a lineup of 8 samples reading the same paragraph (four ElevenLabs voices, two OpenAI voices, one Gemini voice, plus an expressive ElevenLabs model).
- **Look:** picked from three rendered style frames of the same scene: a light brand look, a dark brand look ("Zone night") and a navy-and-gold custom look.

## Plan review (5 questions, Nov 20, about 1:20pm PST)

1. **Framing and timing:** post as soon as possible with live, data-driven verification status ("the computer checks pass so far; human understanding hasn't started"), instead of waiting days for every check or hedging so much that the checks go unmentioned. The narrator never says "proved" in its own voice.
2. **Depth:** smart generalist, with one deeper beat (about 35 seconds) on the actual idea, the chain of theta lifts.
3. **Narrator:** ElevenLabs "George" on the expressive eleven_v3 model. It's consistent enough across separately voiced scenes, if you check the seams.
4. **Visual style:** "Zone night": Cormorant Garamond italic headings, DM Sans body, DM Mono labels, orange accent on warm black.
5. **Review cadence:** show only the polished, QA'd cut, not a rough first cut.
